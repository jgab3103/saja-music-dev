Finger Number Songs

Songs requested by our beginner piano students, arranged so you can learn them before reading music notation.

How it works: Choose a song, put your thumb on the starting key shown, and press the fingers in the order of the big numbers. Finger 1 is your thumb and finger 5 is your little finger.

Listen first, then try it yourself. Longer notes are marked with a hold symbol. You can slow the music down while you practise.
