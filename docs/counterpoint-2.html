Check your work

The checker looks for vertical consonances, parallel perfect intervals, melodic leaps, and a suitable beginning and cadence. It gives guidance rather than automatically rewriting your answer.

First species: the essentials

Use one whole note for each cantus note. On every beat, the voices should form a consonance: a unison, third, fifth, sixth or octave (including compounds). Avoid parallel perfect fifths and octaves; favour contrary and oblique motion. Begin and end on a perfect consonance, approach the final by step, and keep both melodic lines singable. This is a teaching implementation of Fux-style principles, not a complete formalisation of every rule or historical exception.
