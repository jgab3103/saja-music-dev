SAJA MUSIC / CONTACT

We'd love to hear from you.

SAJA Music is a growing collection of free resources for learning, teaching and making music. Whether you've found something useful, spotted a problem or have an idea for something we could create, we'd love your feedback.

Get in touch

Our learning resources are currently in beta, and we're particularly keen to hear from students, musicians and teachers who are willing to try them out. Bug reports, suggestions and feedback on how the activities work are all welcome.

SUPPORT OPEN MUSIC EDUCATION

Help us keep learning free.

SAJA Music is a not-for-profit educational project. We create and share our resources freely, without advertising, subscriptions or paywalls.

If you'd like to support what we're building, we welcome voluntary contributions to help with hosting, development and the ongoing creation of new learning materials.
