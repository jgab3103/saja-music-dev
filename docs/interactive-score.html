Orchestration & Instrumentation · Interactive practice

Orchestral Score Reading Lab

Practise identifying pitches, reading vertical harmonies and reducing orchestral textures onto a grand staff. Build an orchestra from historical presets or customise every staff, clef and section size.

Each generated measure contains four quarter notes in 4/4. Instrument families are grouped with orchestral brackets and connected barlines. The exercises use generated practice material, not quotations from historical scores. Instrument counts in the presets are illustrative starting points, not fixed historical rules.
