Two-Hand Little and Big Steps

Can your left and right hands walk together? Start with tiny finger steps, then try bigger jumps. Keep a steady beat and let your fingers stay curved and relaxed.

Printable two-hand steps worksheet

Keep practising away from the screen with SM_13 — Two Hand Little and Big Steps.

1. Get your hands ready

Put both hands on your piano. Your left-hand little finger is 5 and your right-hand thumb is 1. Both hands will move at the same time.

Try saying “five and one” as you play those two fingers together. Then relax your hands before the next step.

Remember: Finger numbers describe which fingers to play. You can practise these patterns on any comfortable set of five neighbouring white keys for each hand.

2. Choose your steps

Each challenge comes from the worksheet. Select one to practise.
