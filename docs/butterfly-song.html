The Butterfly Song

Make your hands move like butterfly wings! Start with both thumbs on middle C, then move your hands away from each other and back together.

1. Get ready to fly

Put both thumbs (finger 1) on middle C. Your left hand reaches towards lower notes; your right hand reaches towards higher notes.

Left hand 🦋

Finger 1 starts on C. As your fingers move to 2, 3, 4 and 5, the notes get lower.

Right hand 🦋

Finger 1 starts on C. As your fingers move to 2, 3, 4 and 5, the notes get higher.

Keep your fingers curved and relaxed. Both hands play at the same time.

2. Open and close your butterfly wings

Follow the numbers. Both hands use the same finger number at the same time. Choose any circle to hear and see that step.
