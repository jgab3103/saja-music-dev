SAJA MUSIC

Music education, open to everyone.

We create high-quality, freely accessible music education resources that combine practical musicianship, creative exploration and interactive technology to make learning music engaging and enjoyable.

Our growing collection of resources includes interactive learning tools, exercises and teaching materials for students of all ages and skill levels, covering music theory, aural skills, rhythm, composition, improvisation, counterpoint, orchestration, music history, performance, instrumental technique and sound synthesis.

We believe that high quality educational resources should be accessible to everyone. All our resources are freely available, with no advertising, paywalls or subscriptions required.

Just the freedom to learn and explore music.

1 November 2026 BeatMatrix – Now Available on the Web!

We've just released a new web-based version of BeatMatrix! Originally developed as an iOS app, BeatMatrix lets you create complex rhythmic patterns with custom accents, subdivisions, repeats and tempo changes. Perfect for practising Konnokol, odd time signatures, polyrhythms and all kinds of rhythmic adventures. It's free, runs directly in your browser, and there's nothing to install!
